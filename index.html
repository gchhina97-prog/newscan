Before-pump filter (default on): the top section hides coins that already moved, so price must be within your max 15m move (default 1.5%), your max 1h move, 2x that over 4h, under your max 24h move, and funding not crowded (above 0.05%/8h). Top section: shows coins where the latest 15m volume is above normal by at least your minimum dollar amount (default $5M) OR open interest is up 3 checks in a row (15m steps, last hour, above your minimum rise). Coins with both are listed first and tagged BOTH. The dropdown lets you pick: Volume only (volume rising, ignore OI), OI only (OI rising, ignore volume), Volume OR OI, or Volume AND OI. OI history comes from Binance/Bybit coins only. Stocks, ETFs and commodity perps are hidden by default (Crypto only). Combined data: 24h volume is the sum across exchanges, open interest is the sum of Hyperliquid + Bybit + OKX, funding is the average (approx. per 8h). Exch letters: H Hyperliquid, B Binance, Y Bybit, O OKX.
Under each % is the extra dollar volume: latest window volume minus the normal (average) volume, e.g. +$5.2M. Vol % = latest closed window vs the average of earlier windows of the same size (15m vs previous 20 candles, 1h vs previous 24h, 4h vs previous 6 windows, 24h vs the 24h before). +200% means 3x normal. Candles come from Binance first, then Bybit, then Hyperliquid, so the columns fill in over a minute or two (Hyperliquid-only coins are slower).
Pre-pump = big volume spike while the 1h price move is still small. Signal (0 to 7) adds up spikes across timeframes. It finds unusual activity and cannot predict pumps. Use a stop-loss. Not financial advice.
