Vol % = volume of the latest closed window vs the average of earlier windows of the same size. 15m: last candle vs previous 20 candles. 1h: last hour vs previous 24 hours. 4h: last 4 hours vs previous 6 windows. 24h: last 24h vs the 24h before. Example: +200% means 3x normal volume.
Pre-pump = big volume spike while the 1h price move is still small. Signal score (0 to 7) adds up spikes across timeframes. It finds unusual activity, it cannot predict pumps. Use a stop-loss. Not financial advice.
Data: Hyperliquid public API, no key needed. Candle data loads gradually (API rate limit), highest volume coins first, then keeps refreshing hot coins.
